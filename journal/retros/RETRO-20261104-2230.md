## Was the estimate wrong, or was it variance?

near mid per playbook 2026-10-26 rule", but the recorded numbers sat
+0.075 to +0.085 ABOVE the mid. The raw model's spot (~28850) was inferred
from the same day's Kalshi rungs, so it was not an independent level. The
model's "edge" was its sigma and drift on top of the book's own centre.

lean on.

Two gaps in the 10-26 rule let this through:
1. It was written for the self-model sitting BELOW the book on an up-rung
   (it priced too much downside). Here the model sat ABOVE the book, after
   the post-midterm rally. The rule's logic (no independent backing, so
   defer to the book) does not depend on the direction.
2. "Near the mid" had no number. 0.08 is not near on a 0.30 rung.

## Lesson -> playbook edit

The 10-26 equity-index rule now applies in both directions and has a
number: with no independent level (a sourced spot quote taken after the

counts as independent. Put the raw model number in the note.
