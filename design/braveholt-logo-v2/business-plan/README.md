# Braveholt business plan, on one page (Draft 0.1, October 2026)

- `Braveholt-Business-Plan.pdf`: the plan on one page.
- `business-plan-preview.png`: a PNG render of the same page.

## Summary

1. **Positioning.** U.S. small businesses chasing logistics, maintenance and
   supply defense contracts. Each weekly item is one plain line, a note on
   who the award is an opening for, and its source.
2. **Revenue ladder.**
   - Free weekly digest
   - Sponsorship: $300–1,500 per issue, from 2,000 subscribers
   - Field Alerts Pro: $29/mo, or $990/yr for teams, from month 6
   - Advisory and BD research: $2,500–10,000 per project, from month 12
3. **Timeline.**

   | Period | Subscribers | Monthly revenue (assumed) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 0–3 months | 300 | $0 |
   | 3–6 months | 800 | $0 |
   | 6–12 months | 2,000 | $1.5–3k |
   | 12+ months | 5,000 | $5k+ |

4. **AI team operations.** The pipeline runs collect, classify, draft, then
   verify against source. A human approves each issue (about 30 min/week)
   before it is sent. Sales, advisory work and bidding stay with people.
5. **Automation level.**
   - Digest: about 90%
   - Alerts: about 95%
   - Marketing: about 70%
   - Sponsor sales: about 30%
   - Advisory and bidding: about 20%
6. **Risks.** Summaries are a commodity, AI can make errors, legal exposure,
   reaching readers, and missed issues.
7. **Next 30 days.**
   - Fix the niche and its NAICS codes
   - Choose an email service and add a privacy notice
   - Build the first pipeline (needs war.gov and api.usaspending.gov allowed on the network)
   - Run a 4-week trial
   - Consult an attorney

All figures are unvalidated assumptions, to be revised quarterly. The legal
items are general information, not legal advice.
