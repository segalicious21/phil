# Braveholt brand guide v1.0 (October 2026)

- `Braveholt-Brand-Guide.pdf`: the full guide on one long page.
- `brand-guide-preview.png`: a PNG render of the same page.

## Contents

1. **Three layers.** Core never changes: name, mark, colors, type, slogan,
   principles. Story gets a one-line adjustment: About exploded view and hero
   eyebrow. Offer is swapped when the business changes: hero subline, Digest,
   Capabilities, subscribe, disclaimer.
2. **Logo.** Lockups, clear space (1/4 of mark), minimum size (mark 16 px /
   6 mm, horizontal 120 px / 30 mm), and four misuses. The B is negative
   space.
3. **Color.**
   - Ink `#17241D`
   - Bone `#F1EEE6`
   - Brass `#D9A84E` (dark backgrounds)
   - Brass light `#B8862E` (light backgrounds)
   - Paper `#F4F1EA`

   Use brass in one place per view.
4. **Type.**
   - Spectral SC 600 (wordmark, eyebrows)
   - Spectral (headlines)
   - IBM Plex Sans KR (body)
   - IBM Plex Mono (labels, data)
5. **Voice.** Steady, Plain, Exact. Do not use SDVOSB, veteran-owned, 8(a),
   "certified", "official", or government insignia until confirmed.
6. **Website copy map.** Each piece of site copy by layer, with what to do if
   the business changes.
7. **Business-change checklist.**
   - LLC "any lawful purpose"
   - USPTO classes (41 now; consider 35/39/42 intent-to-use)
   - SAM.gov NAICS
   - Keep the disclaimer
   - No government insignia
   - Attorney review

The legal items are general information, not legal advice.
